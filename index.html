EthAir

Turns an ESP32‑S3 into a USB Ethernet adapter that bridges to its own Wi‑Fi. Plug it into a camera or any device with a USB host port, join the Wi‑Fi from your phone, and reach the device as if it were on your network.

Install from this page

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (the browser talks to the board over USB).
  2. Unplug the board (Cardputer: set its power switch OFF).
  3. Hold G0 (other boards: BOOT) and plug a USB‑C data cable from the computer into the board, then release it. The screen stays dark: it is waiting to be flashed.
  4. Click the button for your board, pick the serial port (“USB JTAG/serial debug unit”), confirm.
  5. When it is done, unplug the cable and plug it in again.

M5Stack Cardputer / Cardputer ADV

Status screen, G0 held 5 s = factory reset.

This browser cannot flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https to flash.

Any other ESP32‑S3 board

No screen; a button on GPIO0 held 5 s = factory reset. Needs native USB (not a USB‑serial chip only).

This browser cannot flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https to flash.

Then

  1. Set the device's network to DHCP and plug EthAir into its USB port. Cardputer: keep its own power switch OFF, it runs from the device's port.
  2. Join the Wi‑Fi EthAir‑XXXX, password ethair123.
  3. The device is at 192.168.73.2; settings at http://192.168.73.1.

Updates later go over Wi‑Fi, see the README. Source, documentation and issues: github.com/koorchik/ethair.
